# Visual review

All eight final PNGs were opened and visually inspected on October 29, 2026.
The corresponding SVGs are generated from the same Matplotlib figure state.
Titles, phase panels, axis definitions, displayed uncertainty, categorical
outcomes and head-reference denominators were checked. No clipping of the
data ranges was introduced to hide negative or above-one recovery values.

Specific checks: residual-transfer figure preserves discovery threshold
sensitivity and wide content intervals; head figure labels 64 selected-layer
heads and empirical random p95; lens figure retains very large early logits
and nonmonotonic probability mass; token-group figure retains negative and
above-one effects; control bars begin at zero; joint outcomes are fractions;
the eighth figure distinguishes restricted eight-label mass from semantic
first-token margins. Full interpretive qualifications are in the captions.

Overview group/lens figures omit uncertainty shading where the full tables
are the quantitative record; captions explicitly say so. This is visual
review of analysis figures, not a claim that the historical campaign audit
passed or that the wrapper's original figure paths were correct.
