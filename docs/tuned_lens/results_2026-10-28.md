## 1. Headline

1. **Fit quality.** In every model the Tuned Lens matches the model's final output far better
   than the Logit Lens. Held-out KL at the last translated stage falls from 7.6 to 0.05 (Qwen),
   0.89 to 0.10 (InternVL), 1.10 to 0.03 (LLaVA) and 2.06 to 0.16 (Pali).
2. **M4: the late decision stays.** The large jump in correct-label readability happens at
   the same stage under both lenses: about stage 25 for Qwen, InternVL and LLaVA, and about 29
   for Pali. The Tuned Lens finds no hidden early answer.
3. **Weak early signals.** In two models the Tuned Lens finds a small correct-label signal
   before the jump:
   - **LLaVA:** from stage 20, about 5 stages early, for letters and rare letters. Correct
     probability is about 0.42–0.45 against 0.25 chance.
   - **InternVL:** from stage 22 for rare letters, about 0.39 against 0.25.

   Both signals are small: the margin is about 0.05–0.3 logits, against 1–6 logits at the jump.
4. **M5: label before content holds, and the gap grows.** Under the Tuned Lens the content
   word becomes readable at the same stage or later (InternVL letters: stage 34 against 27).
   For Pali, content is never readable under either lens.
5. **M7: the "default A/B/C/D, then switch" reading weakens.** Under the Logit Lens, the
   requested unusual alphabet gets almost no mass until the decision stage. Under the Tuned
   Lens it already carries substantial mass in the middle layers:
   - **InternVL:** about 0.9 or more from stage 17.
   - **LLaVA:** about 0.5–0.7 from stage 15.
   - **Qwen:** about 0.3–0.5 at stages 13–24.
   - **Pali:** only about 0.1–0.3.

   During those stages, correct-label readability stays at chance. So the middle layers
   linearly encode *which alphabet was requested*, not *which answer*. The late "switch" seen
   under the Logit Lens is partly a feature of that readout. This is the main change to a
   Core-7 conclusion and needs the validation listed in section 7.

The design was frozen before any result, as set out in the plan:
- **Data.** Corpus: CLEVR-MCQ-4 v3 screening + calibration (`dataset_hash f5573e25f3aa2bf2`,
  `corpus_sha256 5251fc18…`), split by scene into 360 train and 60 validation scenes
  (seed 20261027). Each model has 4,320 train and 720 validation prompts.
- **Fit positions.** Rule `text_minus_labels_v1`: prompt text only. It leaves out image tokens,
  the answer position, option labels, the position before each label and the delimiters.
- **Translator.** Zero-initialised affine `h + Ah + b` in fp32, trained on
  KL(final ‖ lens) with SGD Nesterov (lr 0.1, wd 1e-3, linear decay, clip 1.0).
- **Budget.** 250 steps × 16,384 tokens, about 30–37 passes over the fit tokens. The kept
  checkpoint is the one with the best validation KL, checked every 25 steps.
- **Evaluation.** Core-7 behavior split: 256 items, 3,072 prompt variants. Milestones use the
  Core-7 rule (first stage where mean > threshold and bootstrap `ci_low` > threshold).

**Hardware.** Jarvislabs A100-PCIE-40GB, on demand, with torch 2.5.1+cu121 and transformers
4.57.6. The plan's 80 GB spot instance was unavailable.

**Evaluator Logit Lens against released Core-7.** For InternVL and LLaVA, every Logit Lens
milestone from this evaluator matches the released Core-7 discovery tables exactly
(`consolidation/core7_release_results/*/discovery/tables/m4_m5_m7_lens.csv`). The largest
stage-mean difference is ≤ 0.05 logits, or ≤ 0.001 in probability; this reflects GPU and
precision differences. For Qwen, the release has only a figure. The research log gives
letters/rare-letters readable at 23/25, while this run gives 22/24. Qwen's letters margin
here crosses only barely at stage 22 (mean 0.115, `ci_low` 0.052), so the one-stage gap is
probably numerical. The integrator should confirm it against the original Qwen rows. Pali
has no Core-7 baseline because it was ineligible.

In every stage of every model, the best checkpoint was the last one (step 250), and there
was no validation overfitting. The translators would probably improve with more steps. That
matters most for Pali and InternVL, whose middle-stage KL stays around 0.55–0.8 compared with
0.15–0.17 for Qwen and LLaVA. Pali's KL plateaus at stages 18–24: 0.7584 three times. I
checked the checkpoints: the stages differ in the 5th decimal and have distinct histories,
so this is not a copying bug.

## 6. Per-model verdict (plan categories)

| Model | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Qwen | **Preserves the late milestones.** The jump stays at stage 25. Letters cross 2 stages earlier, but only with a margin of about 0.1. Between stages 22 and 27 the Tuned Lens reads *less* correct-label probability than the Logit Lens (e.g. s24 0.42 vs 0.55). Content is delayed to the output. M7: the requested alphabet is partly present in the middle layers. |
| InternVL | **Preserves the decision stage (25).** Rare letters reveal a small earlier signal (s22–24, about 0.39–0.41). Content is delayed (34/31). **Changes M7**: rare-letters requested mass is ≥ 0.8 from stage 17, so the Logit Lens dip at s21–29 is not seen. |
| LLaVA | **Reveals information earlier, though weakly.** Letters and rare letters reach about 0.42–0.45 correct from stage 20, against a jump at 25. Numbers show no such signal. **Changes M7**: requested-alphabet mass is 0.5–0.7 from stage 15, where the Logit Lens shows about 0.00. |
| Pali (provisional) | **Under-fitted; the Tuned Lens adds little.** Both lenses rise at stage 29. At stages 33–41 the Tuned Lens reads *less* than the Logit Lens (letters s38: 0.41 vs 0.54), and they agree only at the output. Content is unreadable in both. The Tuned Lens milestones are weak evidence until the translators fit better and the quirk report is in. |

**Effect on the staged-route hypothesis.** The late, sharp label decision holds in all four
families and is not an artefact of the untrained readout. Neither is label-before-content.
The part that weakens is "the model first defaults to A/B/C/D and switches to the requested
alphabet late." With a learned readout, the requested alphabet can already be read in the
middle layers in Qwen, InternVL and LLaVA. What happens late is the *answer* and its
expression in output vocabulary, not alphabet selection.

## 7. Limits and open items

1. **Pali is provisional.** It is waiting on Yashav's quirk report. If the report changes
   the prompt, readout or stage convention, `paligemma2_10b_mix` has to be rerun (about
   4 h, about ₹330).
2. **The translators are under-trained.** Every stage's best checkpoint is step 250. Longer
   training would be a new, separately named run (e.g. `full_02_steps1000`). It must not
   replace `full_01`.
3. **The M7 change needs validation before it enters shared conclusions.** Planned checks:
   - **Per-item bootstrap:** the milestone CIs reuse Core-7's `per_layer` summary. It
     resamples prompt variants (1,024 per scheme = 256 items × 4 positions) as if they were
     independent. The CIs are therefore comparable to Core-7 but too narrow. The planned
     validation re-bootstraps over items or scenes.
   - **Control:** confirm the mid-depth requested-alphabet mass does not appear when the
     prompt lacks those symbols.
   - **Fit on fewer positions:** check the result is not driven by fit positions next to
     option text.
4. **Small fitting corpus.** About 0.11–0.13 M tokens per model, against about 65 M in the
   paper. Validation KL shows no overfitting, but translators fitted on CLEVR prompts may not
   transfer to other tasks.
5. **Descriptive only.** These are readouts. Causal claims stay with M1/M2/M3/M6.
6. **Deliverables not yet written:** the literature note, the Core-7 readout audit,
   `pali_compatibility.md`, and `validation_<model>.md` (leakage plus per-scene bootstrap).
